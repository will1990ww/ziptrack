Balcony decking in Singapore

Balcony decking turns bare tiles or screed into a warm, usable outdoor room. We supply and install capped WPC (composite) decking, natural Chengal and Balau hardwood, and outdoor marble, porcelain tiles and stone on detailed subframes — with drainage falls, corrosion-resistant fixings and clean edge trims.

Installed balcony decking runs from roughly S$18 per ft² for entry composite (market average near S$27) to above S$45 per ft² for premium hardwood or stone.

Composite (WPC) vs Chengal vs marble & stone

  • Capped WPC / composite: low-maintenance, colour-stable, resistant to rot and termites.
  • Chengal & Balau hardwood: dense tropical timbers, warm grain; weather to silver-grey unless oiled.
  • Outdoor marble, porcelain tiles & stone: cool underfoot, easy to clean — suited to pool surrounds.
Substructure, drainage, access and complex shapes affect price. An itemised quotation after survey is more useful than a board-only rate.

Common questions

Answers before you commit

How much does balcony decking cost in Singapore?
Installed balcony decking commonly starts around S$18 per ft² for entry composite and can exceed S$45 per ft² for premium hardwood or stone, depending on finish, subframe, drainage and access.
Composite or Chengal — which is better?
Capped WPC is lower-maintenance and colour-stable; Chengal is a premium hardwood that can be oiled to retain colour. Both perform well on a ventilated subframe with drainage. We recommend based on your exposure, look and budget.
Do you help with condo (MCST) or HDB approval?
Yes. We provide guidance on approved colours, working-hour restrictions and the documentation your management usually requests. Many condominiums require a renovation/management deposit paid by the home owner to the managing agent (MA) before work starts — typically refunded after inspection. We help prepare the submission; the MA deposit is arranged directly by the home owner.
